# ShuttlePulse AI: Smart Workout & Sports Reflex Planner

**Course:** B.Tech CSE (AIML & Generative AI)  
**Syllabus Tasks:** Mini Project 1 (10.A) & Mini Project 2 (10.B)  
**Live Portfolio & App:** https://kashishthakor1910-dotcom.github.io/kashishthakor.github.io/

## Overview
ShuttlePulse AI is an AI-assisted workout and reaction-speed planning web application tailored for sports players (focusing on badminton footwork/reflexes, cricket agility, and athletic conditioning). It adapts session intensity and recovery based on a player's fatigue score, skill level, and available time, and includes a built-in visual reflex reaction timer.

## Sub-Task Summary (10.A & 10.B)
- **Topic & Content:** Designed structured training readiness rules, drill blocks, recovery steps, and a live reflex speed test.
- **Tools Used:**
  - **Gemini:** Prompt engineering, decision logic structuring, and presentation/content refinement.
  - **Canva:** Workflow/architecture visuals and presentation slides.
  - **GitHub Pages:** Live web hosting and documentation.
- **Testing & Refinements:**
  - High fatigue (`>= 7/10`) automatically switches to low-impact mobility and recovery.
  - Session blocks scale dynamically to fit the selected time (`15`, `30`, or `45` minutes).
  - Reflex pad detects false starts before the green cue.

## Outcomes & Conclusion
Successfully built and published an interactive AI sports planner and reflex tester on GitHub Pages. Future enhancements include wearable heart-rate integration and camera-based footwork tracking.
