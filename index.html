ShuttlePulse AI: Smart Workout & Sports Reflex Planner

An AI-assisted athletic training & reflex reaction planner built with Gemini, Canva, and GitHub Pages.

1. Problem Definition, Objectives & Requirements

Problem Statement: Athletes and college sports players often rely on static workout routines that fail to adjust for daily fatigue, limited session time, or sport-specific reaction speed.

Objective: Design an AI-powered web application that builds customized warm-ups, badminton/sports agility drills, and recovery protocols based on real-time player readiness.

Tech Stack & AI Tools: Gemini (Prompt Engineering & Training Logic), Canva (System Diagrams & Presentation Deck), HTML/CSS/JS + GitHub Pages (Deployment).
